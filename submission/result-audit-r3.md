## Audit method and limits

The 35 unit tests passed. The baseline generator reported its seven analytical artifacts and 1,890 mission rows current. The independent checker rebuilt path geometry and link margins from coordinates and solved the original piecewise mass residual by bisection for every saved row. Calibration, boundaries, sensitivities, feasibility, and mission scripts also passed their --check modes. This establishes numerical consistency of the saved model. It is not flight, installed-radio, command-path, structural, or operational validation.

The NASA Systems Engineering Handbook (NASA/SP-2016-6105 Rev. 2, 2016, sections 4 and 5) supports the paper's distinction between system definition, product verification, and validation. The new workflow figure is an original account of this study's selected steps. It does not claim a completed Vee or NASA process compliance.

## Source recheck

The [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/nasa_systems_engineering_handbook_0.pdf) identifies system design, product verification, and product validation as distinct process areas. Current official [M30](https://enterprise.dji.com/matrice-30/specs), [Matrice 4](https://enterprise.dji.com/matrice-4-series/specs), and [Mavic 3M](https://enterprise.dji.com/mavic-3-m/specs) specifications still show the cited masses, batteries, and maximum hover times. The prior [Doodle Labs OEM page](https://doodlelabs.com/product/oem/) now redirects to a redesigned page; manufacturer-indexed text from the older page still records 102 g, 5 W receive, 14 W peak, and 1.6 W maximum RF. This source drift is disclosed here; it does not turn the unselected payload into tested hardware.

## Disposition

No discrepancy in the saved model results was found. The concrete manuscript error was the outdated test count. The large calibration residuals are real fit differences and remain in the paper with their limits. New architecture prose and the workflow diagram identify decisions supported by the model without adding flight or integration evidence.
